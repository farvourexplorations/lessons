Let's Begin

👋 Hello, Satellite Scientists!

Have you used Google Maps? Watched TV? Seen a weather forecast? All of these use satellites — machines flying high above Earth!

✨ Satellites orbit Earth — they go around and around it! They send and receive signals to help us with weather, GPS, and communication!
