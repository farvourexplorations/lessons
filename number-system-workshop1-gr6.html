The Big Question

Why does dividing make things bigger?

If I divide 6 by 2, I get 3. Smaller. If I divide 6 by ½, I get 12. Bigger. That seems wrong — but it's completely true. Today you'll discover exactly why, and unlock one of the most powerful ideas in all of number theory.

Here's the key question: if you have ¾ of a cup of yoghurt and each serving is ⅜ of a cup — how many servings do you have? Take a guess before we begin. Hold that thought.

The number system at Grade 6 is all about extending what you already know. You've divided whole numbers — now you'll divide fractions. You've added decimals — now you'll do it fluently with any number of digits. You already know more than you think.

🔁 Reciprocal ➗ Quotient 🏆 GCF 🔢 LCM ⚙️ Algorithm
Reciprocal — flip the fraction. The reciprocal of a/b is b/a.

The reciprocal of ¾ is 4/3. The reciprocal of 5 is 1/5. The reciprocal of 1 is 1.

Your turn: what is the reciprocal of 2/7? Of 8? Of ½?
Quotient — the answer you get when you divide two numbers.

In 12 ÷ 4 = 3, the quotient is 3. In ¾ ÷ ½ = 3/2, the quotient is 3/2.

Your turn: what is the quotient of 15 ÷ 3? Of ½ ÷ ¼?
Greatest Common Factor (GCF) — the largest number that divides evenly into two or more numbers.

Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. Factors of 8: 1, 2, 4, 8. GCF = 4.

Your turn: what is the GCF of 18 and 24?
Least Common Multiple (LCM) — the smallest number that both numbers divide into evenly.

Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16... Multiples of 6: 6, 12, 18... LCM = 12.

Your turn: what is the LCM of 5 and 8?
Algorithm — a step-by-step method for solving a problem that always works.

Long division is an algorithm. The steps for multiplying decimals are an algorithm. Algorithms are reliable — follow the steps, get the right answer every time.

Your turn: describe an algorithm you already use for something in everyday life.

🔑 The one rule that unlocks fraction division:

a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c
"Keep · Change · Flip" — keep the first fraction, change ÷ to ×, flip the second fraction

Real-World Connections

These numbers are everywhere 🌍

Fractions, decimals, GCF and LCM aren't just abstract maths — they're the tools professionals use every single day to solve real problems.

👩‍🍳

Cooking & Baking

Halving a recipe means dividing every fraction by 2. Scaling it up to feed 30 people instead of 6 means multiplying fractions. Chefs calculate this every service.

🏗️

Construction

Builders measure in fractions of an inch. Cutting timber to exact lengths means dividing mixed numbers. A mistake of ⅛ inch can make a joint fail.

💊

Medicine

Nurses calculate drug dosages using decimal arithmetic every shift. Getting 0.1mg vs 1mg wrong is the difference between a dose and an overdose.

🎵

Music

Time signatures, note durations, bar lengths — music is built entirely from fractions. A 4/4 time signature means 4 quarter-note beats per bar.
