Before we start

🔑 Two skills you already have

Tap each word. You met all of these already — this lesson just puts them to work.

Common factor Highest common factor (HCF) Simplify Equivalent
Common factor — a number that divides evenly into two other numbers. You found these back in Unit 2.3.

Common factors of 12 and 18: 1, 2, 3, 6
Highest common factor (HCF) — the biggest number from that list.

HCF of 12 and 18 is 6 — the same number you'll use today to simplify fractions in one step.
Simplify — write a fraction using the smallest possible numbers, without changing its value.

12/18 simplifies to 2/3.
Equivalent — equal in value, even though it looks different.

1/2, 0.5, and 50% are all equivalent — three outfits, one value.
