Producers and consumers

All living things need energy. We can sort them by how they get their energy.

🌱 Producers

A producer makes its own food. Plants use light from the Sun to make food in their leaves. All plants are producers.

🐾 Consumers

A consumer cannot make its own food. It must eat other living things. There are three kinds:

Herbivore — eats only plantsCarnivore — eats only animalsOmnivore — eats plants and animals

1a What is a producer?

A producer is a living thing that makes its own food using energy from the Sun. Plants are producers.

Food chains

A food chain is a diagram that shows the order in which animals eat plants and other animals to get energy. We use pictures or words to represent (stand for) each living thing, joined by arrows.

➜ What does the arrow mean?

The arrow points from the food to the animal that eats it. It shows the direction the food moves.

Food has energy in it, so the arrow also shows the direction the energy moves. Read each arrow as “is eaten by”.

📏 Rules for every food chain

✅ It always starts with a producer (a plant).

✅ It has at least one consumer. Most have more than one.

✅ The order is always: producer → consumer, because the consumer eats the producer.

From food chains to a food web

Most animals eat more than one kind of food. A frog eats worms, flies, beetles and other insects. So most animals are part of more than one food chain. When food chains join together, they make a food web. A food web is a more accurate (true to real life) way to show how energy moves between living things.

5 How does a food web help us to understand feeding relationships in nature?

A food web shows that most animals eat more than one kind of food and are eaten by more than one animal. It shows all the food chains in a habitat and how they link together, so it shows what really happens in nature better than one food chain.

2e Predict what would happen to the sea turtles if all the great white sharks were killed.

The number of sea turtles would increase, because nothing would eat them any more. Later, there might not be enough jelly fish for so many turtles, so some turtles could die from lack of food and the number would go down again.

Energy units are example numbers to show the pattern.
☀️ Where does the energy go?

When a herbivore eats a plant, it does not keep all of the energy:

• some energy is used by the herbivore to stay alive
• some energy is stored in its body
• some energy goes into the surroundings as heat

A carnivore that eats the herbivore only gets the stored energy. The rest is lost from the food chain.

📉 The big pattern

Energy is lost at every link. So the amount of energy passed on gets less and less along the chain.

This is why food chains are usually short, and why there are fewer hawks than grasshoppers.

Learner's Book · Questions 1–5, p. 102

1 Where does the energy in a food chain or food web come from?

Model answer: All the energy comes from the Sun.

2 How do producers use this energy?

Producers use the Sun's energy to make their own food.

3 How is the energy in a producer transferred to a consumer?

The consumer eats the producer. The energy stored in the producer's food passes into the consumer's body.

4 How does a consumer, such as a hawk, depend on producers?

The hawk eats the lizard, the lizard eats the grasshopper, and the grasshopper eats the bean plant. All the hawk's energy first came from the producer. Without producers, there would be no food or energy for the hawk.

5a What happens to the amount of energy as it is transferred through a food chain?   5b Say why this happens.

5a The amount of energy gets less and less at each link.
5b Each living thing uses some energy to stay alive and loses some as heat. Only the energy stored in its body is passed on to the next link.

Build a desert food web

📄 The Namib Desert

The Namib Desert stretches for over 1000 km along the coast of Namibia in southern Africa. Very little rain falls there. Many animals live on the sand dunes of the desert. Ants, beetles and termites eat seeds and bits of grass that wind carries from the coast. Antlions eat ants, spiders eat termites, and scorpions eat beetles and spiders. Sunspiders eat scorpions and beetles. Lizards eat spiders, termites, beetles and sunspiders. The sidewinder adder eats lizards.

3c Explain why a food web is more like what happens in nature than a food chain.

In nature, most animals eat more than one kind of food and are eaten by more than one kind of animal. For example, the lizard eats spiders, termites, beetles and sunspiders. A food chain shows only one path, but a food web shows all the links between the living things in a habitat.
