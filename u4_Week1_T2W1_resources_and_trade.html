For the next few weeks, we'll investigate one big question. At the end of the unit, you'll use everything you learn in a Global Resources Debate.

How does the distribution and use of resources influence trade, wealth, and economic development between countries?

🚢

💰

Do resources make a country rich?

Is a country with lots of resources always wealthy? (Not always!)

Check your understanding

What did the Trade Lab teach us?

Scenario: In the oil shortage, Saudi Arabia stopped selling oil. Thailand's car factory stopped. But Saudi Arabia also stopped receiving Thai rice, which it needs because its desert climate makes rice very hard to grow.

Which word best describes this situation?

🇹🇭 Did you know? Thailand's trade in numbers

In 2023, Thailand sold about US$280 billion of goods to other countries. Thailand sells about one-third of all the natural rubber traded in the world, more than any other country, and is the world's second-biggest rice exporter after India. Thailand's biggest import is crude oil.
