SKILL 2 · WATCH OUT

Three traps that lose marks

“Greater than”

“Greater than 3” on 1–8 means 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.
The 3 is not included.

Greater than 6? Tap7, 8 → 2 out of 8

Odd numbers

Odd numbers are 1, 3, 5, 7 …
Many people forget the 1!

Odd on a dice? Tap1, 3, 5 → 3 out of 6

Repeated sections

Red, red, blue, red, green, blue.
Count every red section.

Red? Tap3 out of 6

SKILL 4 · PREDICTING

How many times should it happen?

trials ÷ total outcomes × outcomes you want

Roll a dice 24 times. How many 5s?

Step 1 — tapEach number: 1 out of 6 Step 2 — tap24 ÷ 6 = 4 → expect 4 fives

Roll a dice 24 times. How many even numbers?

Step 1 — tapEven = 2, 4, 6 → 3 out of 6 Step 2 — tap24 ÷ 6 × 3 = 12 even numbers

Expected = what should happen (in theory).

Is expected always the same as experimental?

No, because every result happens by chance.
