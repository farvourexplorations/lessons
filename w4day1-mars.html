We have been to space! Now we zoom in on one special planet — Mars, the Red Planet! Scientists are studying Mars right now with robots called rovers!

✨ Mars is very far away — it takes 7 months to get there by rocket! It is red, cold, rocky and dusty. No humans have been there yet!

Mars Facts

🔴 Discover Mars!

Tap each card to flip it and discover a Mars fact! Read it out loud with your teacher.

🎨 What colour is Mars? Mars is RED! Iron dust makes it look rusty and red. 🔴
🌡️ Is Mars hot or cold? Very COLD! Up to -60°C at night. Brrr! 🥶
📏 Is Mars near or far? Very FAR AWAY! It takes 7 months by rocket! 🚀
🤖 Who explores Mars? ROVERS! Robot vehicles drive on Mars and send pictures back. 🤖
🌱 Is there life on Mars? We don't know yet! Scientists are still looking! 🔬
🌪️ What is the weather like? Very windy with huge dust storms! And NO rain! 🌫️
