What did the Flood Lab teach us?

Scenario: In Mission 3, Nong Top managed to flood Rangsit even though the rain was only light.

How was that possible?

Did you know? 2011 vs 2026

In 2011, Thailand had a much bigger flood. The dam upstream released about 3,700 m³ of water every second, around twice as much as this week (about 1,950 m³ per second). Rangsit was badly flooded for weeks. After 2011, Thailand built more flood walls, pumps and water storage areas, and many of those are working hard right now.

Staying safe · 3 minutes

Safe or not safe?

Geographers don't just study floods. They help people stay safe in them. Decide first, then tap to check. These are real tips for this week at home.

🦺 The three big rules

1. Don't walk, play or ride in floodwater.  2. Stay far away from electricity near water.  3. Listen to the adults at home and official news. In an emergency, an adult can call 1669.
