Compare

Two power stations

Mitochondria and chloroplasts both make energy for the cell, and both are believed to have once been free-living bacteria — an idea called endosymbiotic theory.

The powerhouse 线粒体

Found in almost every eukaryotic cell. Mitochondria break down glucose and release its stored energy as ATP, the molecule cells spend to power everything they do. Muscle cells, which need lots of energy, are packed with mitochondria.

Notice the folded inner membrane (cristae) — more folds means more surface area to make ATP.

The solar panel 叶绿体

Found in plant cells and algae. Chloroplasts capture sunlight and use it to build sugar out of CO₂ and water — the process called photosynthesis. That sugar becomes food for the plant (and for anything that eats the plant!).

The stacked green disks (thylakoids) are where chloroplasts capture sunlight.
Structure and support

What holds a cell together?

Onion cells under a microscope — the thick rectangular outlines are cell walls.

Two kinds of support

Every eukaryotic cell has a cytoskeleton — a network of thin protein fibers that gives the cell its shape, holds organelles in place, and helps the whole cell (or things inside it) move.

Plant cells (like these onion cells) also have a rigid cell wall made of cellulose — the same polysaccharide from our carbohydrates lesson! The wall protects the cell and stops it from bursting when full of water. Animal cells never have a cell wall.

Challenge

Mei and Tom's liver cell puzzle

Mei (美) and Tom are looking at a picture of a liver cell packed with an unusually large amount of smooth ER (endoplasmic reticulum with no ribosomes).

"Liver cells must make a huge amount of protein," says Mei.
"No, Mei — smooth ER isn't mainly about making protein," says Tom.

Who is correct?

Tom is correct. Only rough ER (studded with ribosomes) builds proteins. Smooth ER has no ribosomes — instead it builds lipids and, importantly, helps detoxify drugs and poisons like alcohol. Liver cells are the body's main detox organ, so they are often packed with smooth ER!
Remember

Same organelle family, different jobs: rough ER = protein factory; smooth ER = lipid factory and detox center.
