2Reports

Fact or opinion?

A fact can be checked and proved. An opinion is what someone thinks or feels. Reports use mostly facts.

🔍 Clue: Opinion words often describe feelings or judgements, like beautiful, boring, best, worst, should feel.

2Reports

Personal or impersonal?

Personal 🙋
Uses I, me, my, we, you. Shares feelings. Sounds like someone chatting.
Impersonal 🏛️
No I or you. Uses phrases like It is believed that…, Scientists have found… and the passive voice. Sounds formal.

2Read a news report

Feature hunt 🔎

Read the report first. Then follow the missions and tap the right sentence in the text.

Mission 1 of 7
Warmer Seas Put the World's Coral Reefs in Danger
Science and Environment Desk

A new report from a team of ocean scientists warns that coral reefs around the world are losing their colour faster than ever before. The report was written for governments and world leaders, who make decisions about the environment.

When the sea becomes too warm, corals push out the tiny algae that live inside them and give them their colour. The coral then turns white. This is called coral bleaching. Sea temperatures are rising mainly because of greenhouse gases from burning coal, oil and gas. Pollution from farms and cities is also harming many reefs.

Around half of the world's coral reefs have already been damaged. "If we act now, many reefs can still recover," said Dr Lena Park, who led the research.

The report states that governments must cut greenhouse gases quickly. It also says that countries should protect reefs from pollution and overfishing.

📖 algae = tiny plant-like living things · greenhouse gases = gases that trap heat around the Earth · overfishing = catching too many fish

2Read a news report

Think about the coral report

Type or write your answer first. Then tap to compare with a model answer. Use the report on the last slide to help.

1What type of text is it, and who was the scientists' report written for?
It is a news report about a scientific report. The scientists' report was written for governments and world leaders, who make decisions about the environment.
2Give two reasons why coral reefs are in danger.
1. Sea temperatures are rising because of greenhouse gases from burning coal, oil and gas.
2. Pollution from farms and cities is harming the reefs.
3According to the report, who must do what to help?
Governments must cut greenhouse gases quickly. Countries should protect reefs from pollution and overfishing.
Notice how the answer names WHO and says WHAT they must do.
4Based on the facts, what could you do now to help?
Your own idea is welcome! For example: turn off lights and fans to save energy · walk, cycle or take the bus instead of a car · use less plastic so less pollution reaches the sea · never buy coral as a souvenir.
3Balanced arguments

Read a balanced argument ⚖️

A balanced argument gives reasons for and against an idea, then ends with a conclusion. Tap the coloured words to see the job they do.

Should Homework Be Banned in Primary Schools?

Homework has been part of school life for many years. However, some people now question whether young learners need it at all.

Reasons for a ban

Supporters of a ban argue that children need time to rest, play and be with their families. Some studies suggest that homework has little effect on learning for children under eleven. Furthermore, not every child has a quiet place to work at home.

Reasons against a ban

On the other hand, many teachers believe homework helps children practise skills learned in class. It can also build good habits, such as planning time and working independently. In addition, homework lets parents see what their children are learning.

Conclusion

In conclusion, there are strong points on both sides. Perhaps the best answer is a small amount of useful homework, rather than none at all.

💬Explain to someone at home: what is the difference between a report and a balanced argument?
A report gives facts about one topic to inform the reader. A balanced argument also uses facts, but it gives reasons for and against an idea and ends with a conclusion.
