Why Is a Negative
× a Negative Positive? ✖️

The Big Question

Can two negatives really make a positive?

You lose $5 a day for 3 days: that's 3 × (−5) = −15. Makes sense. But what does (−3) × (−5) mean? You can't do something "negative three times"… or can you? Mathematicians didn't just invent the rule — it's the only answer that keeps the patterns working.

Think about a video of water draining out of a tank at 5 litres per minute (a change of −5 each minute). If you play the video backwards for 3 minutes (−3), does the water level go up or down? That's (−3) × (−5).

Discover the Vocabulary

✖️ Product ➗ Quotient 🔀 Distributive Property 🛑 Terminating Decimal 🔁 Repeating Decimal
Product — the answer to a multiplication.

The product of −4 and 2.5 is −10.
Quotient — the answer to a division. Division by zero is undefined.

The quotient of −18 and −3 is 6, because −3 × 6 = −18.
Distributive Property — a(b + c) = ab + ac. It's the key to proving why (−1)(−1) = 1.

−3(4 + (−4)) = −3 × 0 = 0, so −12 + (−3)(−4) must equal 0.
Terminating Decimal — a decimal that ends.

3/8 = 0.375. The long division reaches a remainder of 0.
Repeating Decimal — a decimal where a block of digits repeats forever. Shown with a bar.

2/11 = 0.181818… = 0.18. A remainder repeats, so the digits repeat.

🔑 Every rational number is a fraction of integers — so it's either a terminating or a repeating decimal. Always.

You'll see why in Investigation 3: when you divide by d, there are only d possible remainders… so eventually one has to repeat (or hit zero).

Investigation 2 · 7.NS.A.2b–c

➗ Division Follows the Same Rules

Division undoes multiplication: a ÷ b = c because b × c = a. So the sign rules carry over exactly. That also means a negative sign can sit in three places in a fraction: −(p/q) = (−p)/q = p/(−q).

Real-World Connections · 7.NS.A.3

Rates of change in the real world 🌍

Whenever something changes at a steady rate — up or down — you multiply or divide signed numbers.

🤿

Diving

A diver descending at −1.5 m per second for 12 seconds changes depth by −1.5 × 12 = −18 m.

📉

Stock Market

A share that loses $2.40 a day for 5 days changes by −$12. Dividing a total change by days gives the average daily change.

🏔️

Mountains

Air temperature drops about 6.5°C per kilometre you climb — a negative rate times a distance.

🔋

Batteries

A phone losing 8% per hour: how long until it falls 60%? That's −60 ÷ (−8) = 7.5 hours.
