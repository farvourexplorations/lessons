Station 1

Poetic licence

Poets are allowed to break the rules of language. But they do it on purpose, for a reason.

What is poetic licence?

Think first, then tap.

Poetic licence is the freedom a poet has to change or break the rules of language (spelling, punctuation, capital letters, grammar, layout) for effect. A licence is permission.

Spot the poetic licence

Tap each line. Which rule is broken? Why might the poet do that?

Explain it like a poet

A strong answer says what the poet did and why.

The poet uses poetic licence by ______. This makes the reader ______ / This shows that ______.

In According to My Mood (Learner’s Book 6.1), the poet uses wrong spelling and punctuation on purpose. This makes a big visual impact and shows that poetry does not have to follow the normal rules: he writes according to his mood.

Cinquain facts

Where does the word cinq come from? What does it mean?

Cinq is a French word meaning five (say it: sank). A cinquain is a poem with five lines.

Two kinds of cinquain

How do lines 1 and 5 connect?

Storm
Dark, wild
Crashing, flashing, roaring
Makes my heart race
Tempest
Both are about the same topic. Both are nouns, and Tempest is a synonym of Storm. Line 1 introduces the topic; line 5 concludes it.
