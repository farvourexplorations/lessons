The Big Question

When do two things add up to nothing?

You earn $15 and spend $15. A diver goes down 20 m and swims up 20 m. A hydrogen atom has one proton (+1) and one electron (−1). In each case the total is zero. Numbers that cancel like this are called opposites.

The temperature at dawn is −7°C. By noon it has risen 12 degrees. Is it above or below zero? And if it then drops 9 degrees by night — where does it end up? By the end of today, you'll solve this in your head.

🧠 Warm-up — does each situation end at zero?

Every number has an opposite the same distance from zero on the other side. Together they make a zero pair. This one idea explains every rule for adding and subtracting signed numbers.

Discover the Vocabulary

📚 Words number explorers use

Click each word to reveal its meaning.

🔢 Integer ↔ Opposite 0️⃣ Additive Inverse 📏 Absolute Value ½ Rational Number
Integer — a whole number or its opposite: …, −3, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3, …

−15 and 42 are integers. 2.5 and ⅓ are not.
Opposite — the number the same distance from 0 on the other side.

The opposite of 6 is −6. The opposite of −2.5 is 2.5. The opposite of 0 is 0.
Additive Inverse — another name for the opposite, because a number plus its additive inverse equals 0.

9 + (−9) = 0. These two make a zero pair.
Absolute Value — a number's distance from 0. It is never negative.

|−8| = 8 and |8| = 8. The distance between two numbers a and b is |a − b|.
Rational Number — any number that can be written as a fraction a/b of integers (b ≠ 0). Includes integers, fractions, terminating and repeating decimals, and their opposites.

−¾, 2.6, −5 and 0.333… are all rational.

🔑 Two big ideas for today

Adding = moving
p + q: start at p, move |q| units — right if q is positive, left if q is negative.
Subtracting = adding the opposite
p − q = p + (−q). And |p − q| is the distance between them.

➖ Subtracting = Adding the Opposite

Every subtraction can be rewritten as an addition: p − q = p + (−q). And the distance between two numbers on the line is the absolute value of their difference, |p − q|.

Real-World Connections

Positive and negative everywhere 🌍

Signed numbers describe direction and change: above and below, gain and loss, forward and back.

🌡️

Weather

Temperatures rise and fall across zero. Meteorologists add and subtract signed changes every day.

🏦

Money

Deposits are positive, withdrawals are negative. A bank balance below zero is an overdraft.

🌊

Elevation

Sea level is zero. Mountains are positive, ocean trenches negative. The distance between them uses |a − b|.

⚛️

Science

Protons (+1) and electrons (−1) cancel to make neutral atoms — real-life zero pairs.
