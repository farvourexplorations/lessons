Learn · Modelled example

Tenths of an hour

Decimals are built on 10s. An hour is built on 60. So what happens when we cut one hour into 10 equal pieces?

💵 1 dollar = 100 cents

0.5 dollars = 50 cents. The digits match because money goes in 100s.

🕒 1 hour = 60 minutes

0.5 hours is not 50 minutes. Half of 60 is 30, so 0.5 hours = 30 minutes.

Choose how many tenths of an hour:

0 min30 min60 min
0.0 hours = 0 minutes
60 minutes ÷ 10 = 6 minutes in each tenth
Google Meet moment · Chant it

Unmute together and count in 6s as the clock fills up: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30… all the way to 60!

Learn · Worked example

Convert 3.7 hours into hours and minutes

Split the time into whole hours and part of an hour. The whole hours stay the same. Only the decimal part needs changing into minutes.

Worked example
1
3.7 hours = 3 whole hours + 0.7 of an hour
Separate the whole number from the decimal part.
2
Change 0.7 of an hour into minutes
Method A · multiply by 60
0.7 × 60 = 42
There are 60 minutes in 1 hour.
Method B · tenths × 6
7 × 6 = 42
0.1 of an hour = 6 minutes, so 7 tenths = 7 × 6 minutes.
3
3.7 hours = 3 hours and 42 minutes ✅
Check: 42 minutes is a bit less than 45 minutes (¾ hour), and 0.7 is a bit less than 0.75. That makes sense!
!
⚠️ Trap: 3.7 hours is NOT 3 hours and 7 minutes
The digit 7 means 7 tenths of an hour, not 7 minutes.

Marathon challenge · Smaller units

Minutes into hours, minutes and seconds

It's the same idea with smaller units. 1 minute = 60 seconds, so 0.1 of a minute = 6 seconds. And every 60 minutes makes one whole hour.

0.05 min3 seconds
0.1 min6 seconds

🏃 Top tips for the marathon

1. The fastest runner has the smallest time. Order the times first.  2. Take away 60 minutes for each whole hour: 120 minutes = 2 hours, 180 minutes = 3 hours. Check which one fits!  3. Change the decimal part of a minute into seconds (× 60).
