The Big Question

Can every number be written as a fraction?

Draw a square with sides of 1 metre. Measure the diagonal. It's about 1.414 metres... but exactly? The ancient Greeks discovered that no fraction, however huge, gives that length exactly. The discovery was so shocking that legend says it was kept a secret.

Type 1 ÷ 7 into a calculator: 0.142857142857... The six digits repeat forever. Now try √2: 1.41421356237... — no repeating block ever appears, even after trillions of digits. What makes these two numbers so different?

🧠 Warm-up — rational or irrational?

Every number on the number line is either rational (a ratio of integers) or irrational. Together they make the real numbers.

Discover the Vocabulary

📚 The language of real numbers

Click each word to reveal its meaning.

➗ Rational Number ♾️ Irrational Number 🛑 Terminating Decimal 🔁 Repeating Decimal √ Square Root
Rational Number — any number that can be written as a/b, where a and b are integers and b ≠ 0. Its decimal either ends or repeats.

5 = 5/1, −0.75 = −3/4, 0.333... = 1/3
Irrational Number — a real number that cannot be written as a fraction of integers. Its decimal goes on forever with no repeating block.

√2, √5, π, 0.1010010001...
Terminating Decimal — a decimal that ends. Happens when the simplified denominator has only 2s and 5s as prime factors.

3/8 = 0.375 (8 = 2×2×2)
Repeating Decimal — a decimal with a block of digits that repeats forever. We draw a bar over the block.

5/11 = 0.45 = 0.454545...
Square Root — √n is the non-negative number that multiplies by itself to give n. If n isn't a perfect square, √n is irrational.

√49 = 7 (rational) · √50 ≈ 7.07 (irrational)

🔁 Why Fractions End or Repeat

Real-World Connections

Irrational numbers all around you 🌍

Irrational numbers aren't rare. They show up whenever we measure diagonals, circles and growth.

📺

Screen Sizes

A TV's size is its diagonal. A 16 by 9 screen has a diagonal of √337 units, an irrational length.

🍕

Circles

Every circle's circumference ÷ diameter is π. Engineers use 3.14 or 22/7 as rational approximations.

🎹

Music

Each piano key's frequency is the one before times the 12th root of 2, an irrational number ≈ 1.0595.

📄

Paper Sizes

A4 paper's length ÷ width is √2, so folding it in half keeps the same shape.
