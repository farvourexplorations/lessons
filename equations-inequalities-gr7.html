The Big Question

How do you find a number you can't see?

You have $60. Movie tickets cost $12.50 each, plus a $4 booking fee for the whole order. How many tickets can you buy? You could guess and check… or write 12.5t + 4 ≤ 60 and solve it in seconds.

Here's a strange fact: if −2x > 6, then x is less than −3, not greater. Dividing by a negative flips the inequality. By the end of today you'll know exactly why.

🧠 Warm-up — which estimate is closest?

Good problem-solvers do three things: estimate first, solve carefully, then check that the answer is reasonable.

Discover the Vocabulary

📚 Words equation-solvers use

Click each word to reveal its meaning.

↩ Inverse Operation 2️⃣ Two-Step Equation ≥ Inequality 🎯 Solution Set 🤔 Reasonable
Inverse Operation — the operation that undoes another. Addition ↔ subtraction, multiplication ↔ division.

To undo "× 3 then + 4", do "− 4 then ÷ 3" — reverse order!
Two-Step Equation — an equation like px + q = r or p(x + q) = r that takes two inverse operations to solve.

3x + 4 = 19 → 3x = 15 → x = 5
Inequality — a statement using <, >, ≤ or ≥. It usually has infinitely many solutions.

12.5t + 4 ≤ 60 means the total can be 60 or less.
Solution Set — all the values that make an inequality true, shown on a number line.

x > 3: an open circle at 3 and an arrow to the right.
Reasonable — an answer that makes sense in the situation. You can't buy 4.48 tickets!

Always ask: Is the answer about the size I estimated? Does it fit the context?

🔑 One rule that's different for inequalities

When you multiply or divide BOTH sides by a NEGATIVE number, flip the inequality sign.
Why? 2 < 5, but −2 > −5. Multiplying by −1 reverses the order on the number line.

Real-World Connections

Equations and limits everywhere 🌍

Budgets, speed limits, fees and goals are all equations or inequalities in disguise.

🎟️

Booking Tickets

Price per ticket × number + booking fee ≤ budget. Websites solve this inequality every time you check out.

📱

Phone Plans

Monthly fee + cost per GB = total bill. Solve to find how much data you used.

🚗

Road Trips

How many hours at 65 mph to go at least 300 miles? 65h ≥ 300.

🏋️

Fitness Goals

Already ran 4.5 km, run 1.5 km a day: how many days to reach 30 km?
