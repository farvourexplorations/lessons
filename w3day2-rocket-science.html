👋 Hello, Rocket Scientists!

Yesterday we launched our rocket. But why does it move? Today we find out using something surprising — a balloon!

✨ When air pushes out of a balloon, the balloon moves the other way! Rockets work the same way — fuel burns and pushes the rocket UP!

🙋 Warm-Up: Push and Move!

Stand up. Push both hands forward — your body moves back! That's how rockets work!
