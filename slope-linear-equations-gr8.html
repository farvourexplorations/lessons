The Big Question

How do you measure steepness?

A wheelchair ramp can rise at most 1 inch for every 12 inches of length. A ski slope, a roof and a road sign showing "8% grade" are all describing the same idea: how much you go up for every step across.

Pick any two points on a straight line and work out rise ÷ run. Now pick two different points. You get the same answer every time. Why can't a straight line be steeper in one place than another?

🧠 Warm-up — proportional or not?

A proportional relationship is a line through the origin, y = mx. Its unit rate and its slope are the same number.

Discover the Vocabulary

📚 The language of lines

📐 Slope ⏱️ Unit Rate 🎯 y-intercept 🔺 Similar Triangles ⚖️ Number of Solutions
Slope (m) — the steepness of a line: rise ÷ run, or change in y ÷ change in x, between any two points.

From (1, 3) to (4, 9): slope = 6 ÷ 3 = 2
Unit Rate — how much y changes for 1 unit of x. In a proportional relationship it equals the slope.

$36 for 3 hours → $12 per hour
y-intercept (b) — where the line crosses the y-axis, the value of y when x = 0.

y = 2x + 5 crosses at (0, 5)
Similar Triangles — same shape, different size. Their sides are in the same ratio. Slope triangles on one line are always similar.

rise/run = 2/1 = 4/2 = 6/3
Number of Solutions — a linear equation can have one solution (x = a), no solution (a = b, false), or infinitely many (a = a, always true).

2x + 1 = 2x + 5 → 1 = 5 → no solution

Real-World Connections

Slope in the real world 🌍

Rates and slopes are everywhere people build, earn, travel and measure.

♿

Ramps

Accessible ramps need a slope of at most 1/12: one unit up for every twelve across.

🏠

Roofs

Builders call a roof that rises 6 inches per foot a "6:12 pitch". That's a slope of 1/2.

🚕

Taxi Fares

$3 to start plus $2 per mile: y = 2x + 3. The slope is the rate, the intercept is the starting fee.

⛰️

Road Signs

An "8% grade" means the road rises 8 m for every 100 m across: slope 0.08.
