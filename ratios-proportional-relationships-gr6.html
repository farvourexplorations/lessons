The Big Question

What do pizza and maps have in common?

A recipe says use 2 cups of flour for every 3 cups of sugar. A map says 1 cm = 50 km. A car travels 60 miles per hour. What do all of these have in common? They're all ratios — and today you'll learn to use them like a mathematician.

A ratio is a way of comparing two quantities. It doesn't just say how much of something there is — it says how much of one thing there is relative to another. That tiny shift in thinking is one of the most useful tools in all of mathematics.

Ratios can be written three ways: as a fraction (3/2), with a colon (3:2), or in words (3 to 2). They all mean the same thing — and today you'll use all three.

Discover the Vocabulary

📚 Words mathematicians use

Click each word to reveal what it means. Then think of your own real-world example before reading ours.

⚖️ Ratio 🔁 Equivalent Ratios 💲 Unit Rate 📐 Proportion 💯 Percent
Ratio — a comparison of two quantities using division.
Written as: a:b or a/b or "a to b"

Example: In a class of 12 boys and 8 girls, the ratio of boys to girls is 12:8, which simplifies to 3:2.

Your turn: Write a ratio you can see right now in your classroom.
Equivalent Ratios — ratios that represent the same relationship, just scaled up or down.

Example: 1:2, 2:4, 3:6, and 10:20 are all equivalent — they all say "one for every two."

Your turn: Write three equivalent ratios to 2:5.
Unit Rate — a ratio where the second quantity is 1. It tells you "how much per one."

Example: $3 per apple. 60 miles per hour. 12 words per minute. The "per 1" makes it a unit rate.

Your turn: If you earn $45 for 9 hours of work, what is your unit rate (per hour)?
Proportion — a statement that two ratios are equal.
Written as: a/b = c/d

Example: 3/4 = 6/8 is a proportion — both ratios represent the same relationship.

Your turn: Is 2/3 = 8/12 a proportion? How do you know?
Percent — a ratio that always compares to 100. "Per cent" means "per hundred."

Example: 30% means 30 out of every 100. It's just a special ratio: 30/100.

Your turn: What percent is the same as the ratio 3:4? (Hint: how many out of 100?)

🔍 Three ways to write the same ratio — which do you prefer?

6
Real-World Connections

Ratios are everywhere 🌍

You've been using ratios your whole life without realising it. Scales on maps, ingredients in recipes, the score of a game — everything you compare is a ratio.

🍳

Cooking & Recipes

Every recipe is a ratio. 2 cups flour : 1 cup butter. Scaling a recipe up or down means finding equivalent ratios — exactly what you just practised.

🗺️

Maps & Scale

A map scale of 1:50,000 means 1cm on paper = 50,000cm in real life. Every map, blueprint, and model uses this ratio thinking.

📈

Finance & Shopping

Interest rates, discounts, tax, tips — all percentages, all ratios. Understanding this gives you real power over money decisions.

⚽

Sport Statistics

Batting averages, goals per game, win ratios — sport is obsessed with ratios. Every sports analyst is a ratio expert.
