Key vocabulary

The parts of a circle

Tap a word below to see which part of the circle it means.

Centre

The middle point of the circle. Every point on the edge is the same distance from here.

Radius

The distance from the centre to the edge of the circle.

Diameter

A straight line from one side of the circle to the other, passing through the centre.

Circumference

The perimeter — the distance all the way around the circle.

Key relationship

Diameter = 2 × radius

Arun draws a circle with radius 3 cm. Sofia draws a circle with diameter 60 mm. Are their circles the same size?

Remember

diameter = radius × 2

radius = diameter ÷ 2

Remember: 1 cm = 10 mm
Check it

Arun's radius is 3 cm, so his diameter is 6 cm = 60 mm. Sofia's diameter is 60 mm. Their circles are exactly the same size!

Worked example

Drawing a circle accurately

Follow these steps to draw a circle of any radius using compasses.

Open your compasses to the radius length, using a ruler to measure.
Mark a dot to show the centre of the circle.
Place the point of the compasses exactly on the dot.
Turn the compasses carefully all the way round to draw the circle.
Tip

Hold the top of the compasses lightly and keep the point still — if it slips, the centre moves and the circle won't be accurate.
