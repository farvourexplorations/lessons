The Big Question

When are two quantities secretly locked together?

If 2 tacos cost $5, then 4 tacos cost $10 and 6 tacos cost $15. Every price is the number of tacos times 2.5. That 2.5 is a hidden constant — and once you know it, you can price any order instantly.

A snail crawls ½ mile in ¼ hour. How fast is that in miles per hour? Your brain might say "½ mile"… but the real answer is 2 miles per hour. By the end of today you'll be able to explain exactly why.

🧠 Warm-up — is each table proportional?

A relationship is proportional when one quantity is always the other multiplied by the same number. That number is called the constant of proportionality, and in Grade 7 you'll find it hiding in tables, graphs, equations and stories.

⏱ Unit Rate ➗ Complex Fraction 📈 Proportional 🔑 Constant k 💯 Percent Change
Unit Rate — how much of one quantity for exactly 1 of another.

$12 for 4 notebooks → $12 ÷ 4 = $3 per notebook.

Your turn: 150 km in 2 hours is how many km per hour?
Complex Fraction — a fraction with a fraction in its numerator, denominator, or both.

(½) ÷ (¼) can be written as a complex fraction. Divide by multiplying by the reciprocal: ½ × 4 = 2.

Your turn: what is (¾) ÷ (½)?
Proportional Relationship — y is always x times the same number. Its table has equal ratios y/x, and its graph is a straight line through the origin (0, 0).

Cost of apples at $2/lb is proportional. A taxi with a $3 starting fee is NOT — the graph doesn't start at 0.
Constant of Proportionality (k) — the number in y = kx. It equals the unit rate, the ratio y/x, and the y-value at the point (1, k) on the graph.

y = 2.5x → every x is multiplied by 2.5. The graph passes through (1, 2.5).
Percent Change — how much something grew or shrank compared with where it started.

Percent change = (new − original) ÷ original × 100%. A $40 game on sale for $30 changed by −25%.

Real-World Connections

Proportions run the world 🌍

Every receipt, recipe, map and speedometer depends on the ideas you used today. Here's where the hidden constant shows up.

🛍️

Shopping

Sales tax is proportional: tax = rate × price. Stores use markups to set prices and discounts to sell more — all percents.

🗺️

Maps & Models

A map scale like 1 cm : 5 km is a constant of proportionality. Architects and game designers use the same idea.

💊

Medicine

Nurses calculate doses per kilogram of body weight — a unit rate. A small error in the constant can be dangerous.

🏦

Banking

Simple interest = principal × rate × time. Savings accounts and loans start with this proportional idea.
