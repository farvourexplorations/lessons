The Big Question

Can you measure a lucky guess?

Here's something wild: I'm going to flip a coin 10 times. You cannot know exactly how many heads will come up — but mathematicians can say something precise and true about it anyway. How? Let's find out.

Think about a time something happened that you didn't expect. Maybe you rolled the exact number you needed. Maybe it rained on the one day you forgot your umbrella. That feeling of surprise? That's probability talking.

🧠 Quick think — drag your gut-feeling answer:

Probability lives between 0 (impossible) and 1 (certain). Everything interesting is somewhere in between. Today we're learning to find those numbers.

Discover the Vocabulary

Words mathematicians use

Click each word below to reveal what it means. Can you think of your own example for each one?

🚫 Impossible 🎲 Unlikely ⚖️ Even Chance ✅ Likely 💯 Certain
Impossible — P = 0
It cannot happen. No matter what. Rolling a 7 on a normal 6-sided die is impossible.

Your turn: think of something that's completely impossible to happen today.
Unlikely — P is close to 0
It could happen, but it would really surprise you. Rolling a 6 three times in a row is unlikely — but possible!

Your turn: what's something unlikely that has actually happened to you?
Even Chance — P = ½ = 0.5 = 50%
Heads or tails? Both equally likely. A fair coin flip is a perfect example of evens.

Your turn: can you design something else that has an even chance?
Likely — P is close to 1
You'd be surprised if it didn't happen. Drawing a non-ace from a card deck is likely — there are only 4 aces in 52 cards!

Your turn: what's something likely to happen to you tomorrow?
Certain — P = 1
It will definitely happen — every single time. Drawing any card from a full deck is certain.

Your turn: name something that is absolutely 100% certain today.

5
Real-World Connections

Probability is everywhere 🌍

Now that you understand the maths, you'll start seeing probability all around you. It's the secret language behind some of the most important decisions in the world.

🌦️

Weather Forecast

"70% chance of rain" — that means in 100 days with the same conditions, it rained on about 70 of them. Not a promise. A calculated probability.

🎮

Video Games

Every loot box, every random encounter, every critical hit — game designers set exact probabilities to make games feel exciting but fair.

🏥

Medicine

Doctors use probability to test whether treatments actually work — or whether the results were just chance. Probability protects us from wishful thinking.
