The Big Question

What happens when you unfold a box?

A cardboard box is a 3D shape. But before it was a box, it was a flat sheet of cardboard, folded along precise lines. That flat shape is called a net. And how much space the box holds is called its volume. Both ideas are hiding inside every package you've ever seen.

Imagine a box that is 4 cm long, 3 cm wide, and 2 cm tall. How many 1 cm cubes could you fit inside it? And if you cut it apart along its edges and laid it flat, what shape would you get? Hold those thoughts — today you'll find out exactly.

🧠 Warm-up — true or false?

Volume and surface area are two completely different measurements. A box can have a large volume (lots of space inside) but a small surface area (not much material to make it), or vice versa. Understanding the difference is what makes you a better designer, builder, and mathematician.

Discover the Vocabulary

📦 Volume 🎁 Surface Area 🔲 Net 🏗️ Rectangular Prism 🔷 Face, Edge & Vertex
Volume — the amount of 3D space inside a shape, measured in cubic units (cm³, m³, in³).

A box with V = 24 cm³ can hold exactly 24 cubes that are 1 cm × 1 cm × 1 cm. Volume tells you capacity.

Your turn: name two real-world situations where you'd need to calculate volume.
Surface Area — the total area of all the faces of a 3D shape, measured in square units (cm², m²).

Surface area tells you how much material you need to cover the outside. Wrapping paper, paint, fabric — all surface area problems.

Your turn: why would a gift-wrapper care about surface area and not volume?
Net — a 2D flat shape that can be folded up to form a 3D figure.

A cube's net consists of 6 equal squares arranged so they fold into the cube. There are actually 11 different ways to arrange a cube's net!

Your turn: how many faces does a rectangular prism have? What shapes are they?
Rectangular Prism — a 3D shape with 6 rectangular faces, all at right angles. Also called a cuboid.

A cereal box, a brick, a book, a room — all rectangular prisms. The special case where all sides are equal is a cube.

Your turn: find 3 rectangular prisms in your classroom right now. What are their approximate dimensions?
Face, Edge & Vertex — the three types of parts of any 3D shape.

Face = a flat side. Edge = where two faces meet. Vertex = a corner point where edges meet. A rectangular prism has 6 faces, 12 edges, and 8 vertices.

Your turn: how many faces, edges, and vertices does a cube have?

🔑 The two key formulas for today:

V = l × w × h
Volume = length × width × height
Units: cm³
SA = sum of all faces
Surface Area = 2(lw + lh + wh)
Units: cm²

Real-World Connections

Volume & Surface Area everywhere 🌍

These aren't abstract calculations — every time someone designs a room, ships a package, fills a pool, or wraps a gift, they're using exactly what you practised today.

📦

Shipping & Packaging

Shipping companies charge by volume (how much space a box takes). Manufacturers minimise surface area (less cardboard = cheaper). These two goals are always in tension.

🏊

Swimming Pools

Volume tells you how many litres of water you need to fill a pool. Surface area tells you how much tile you need to line it. Both calculations matter — and both are expensive to get wrong.

🏗️

Architecture

Architects calculate the volume of rooms (for heating and air conditioning) and the surface area of walls (for insulation and paint). Every building starts with these calculations.

🎁

Gift Wrapping

How much wrapping paper do you need? That's a surface area problem. And the classic maths puzzle: what shape of box holds the most with the least wrapping? Volume vs surface area.
